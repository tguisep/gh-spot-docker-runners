# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person on the other end is trying to do something reasonable, and that
they know things you do not.

## What is expected

- Engage with what someone actually said, not the weakest version of it.
- Accept that a disagreement about code is not a disagreement about a person.
- Say when you are unsure. It is more useful than confident guessing.
- Take corrections without ceremony, and give them the same way.

## What is not

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, or unwelcome attention. Publishing anyone's
private information. Persistently derailing a discussion after being asked to stop.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, commits, and anywhere else this project is being discussed.

## Reporting

Email <thomas82710@gmail.com>. Reports are read by the maintainer and not shared further.

Whatever you report, expect to be believed and asked what outcome you want, rather than
argued with. Where a report concerns the maintainer, say so and it will be handled by
stepping back from the decision rather than by adjudicating it.

## Enforcement

In order: a private word, a public correction, a temporary block, a permanent one. Which one
depends on severity and on whether it has happened before.

---

This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to what a project this size can actually promise to do.
